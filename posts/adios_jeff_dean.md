# Adios Jeff Dean

It is the end of an era. Jeff Dean is leaving Google. Long live Demis Hassabis.

I have long looked up to Jeff Dean as a beacon of engineering excellence. Hearing that he will no longer be a driving force at Google is somewhat saddening, but I am quite excited to see what he does at Discovery Loop.

In celebration, I suggest you read through [this compiled list of Chuck Norris-style Jeff Dean facts.](https://github.com/LRitzdorf/TheJeffDeanFacts)

Here are a few, to give you an idea of Jeff Dean's reputation:

- Jeff Dean compiles and runs his code before submitting, but only to check for compiler and CPU bugs.
- Jeff Dean invented BigTable because his resume was too big to fit anywhere else.
- Jeff Dean doesn't use ECC memory. He anticipates cosmic rays and uses them to improve performance.

If you are interested in a real-life montage of Jeff Dean's numerous accomplishments at Google over the years, I suggest you listed to Acquired's wonderful episodes about the company. It's a long listen, but trust me, it's worth it.
