# Harper's Architecture — Presenting to ACM @ Mines

Earlier this week, I was asked to give a talk to the chapter of the Association of Computing Machinery at Mines.
It was an interesting experience, especially since it ended up being so conversational.

In my talk, I go deep into [Harper's](https://writewithharper.com) fundamental architecture.
I focused on the specific aspects that make it possible to do our grammar checking offline on extremely low power devices.

I imagine it would be quite useful for potential new contributors to the project, so I'll make sure to link to it from the Harper documentation.

<video controls src="/videos/harper_arch.mp4"/>

This is definitely not the best talk I've ever given, and in the video we go on a number of tangents that might bore some viewers.
You've been warned.
